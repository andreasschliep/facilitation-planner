Workshop title: Testing Fundamentals
Duration: 60 min
Start: 14:00
Description: A hands-on introduction to unit testing: what makes a test useful, how to structure it, and why it should fail before it passes.

Activity: 1 Check-in: the bug that got away
Duration: 4 min
Type: Opening
Method: Chat waterfall: everyone types one sentence, all send at once
Purpose: Connect the topic to bugs participants have already met.
4C Phase: C1 Connections

Activity: 2 What makes a test useful?
Duration: 6 min
Type: Discussion
Method: Pairs for three minutes, then three answers in plenary
Purpose: Surface what participants already believe about tests.
4C Phase: C1 Connections

Activity: 3 Anatomy of a unit test
Duration: 8 min
Type: Input
Method: Short talk with live code: arrange, act, assert
Purpose: Give one structure that every test in the session follows.
4C Phase: C2 Concepts
Learning Objectives: LO1

Activity: 4 Story: the test that always passed
Duration: 7 min
Type: Story
Method: One punch, one stumble, one takeaway
Purpose: Show why a test that was never seen failing proves nothing.
4C Phase: C2 Concepts
Learning Objectives: LO2

Activity: 5 Live demo: red, green, refactor
Duration: 10 min
Type: Demo
Method: Facilitator codes; participants call the next step and predict each result
Purpose: Make the test-first rhythm visible before participants try it.
4C Phase: C2 Concepts
Learning Objectives: LO2, LO3

Activity: 6 Pair exercise: first failing test
Duration: 12 min
Type: Exercise
Method: Pairs in breakout rooms with a prepared practice repository
Purpose: Apply arrange-act-assert to a small function until the test passes.
Description: Each pair gets a function with a known bug. Write a test that exposes it, watch it fail, fix the function, watch the test pass.
4C Phase: C3 Concrete Practice
Learning Objectives: LO1, LO3

Activity: 7 Gallery: one test per pair
Duration: 3 min
Type: Debrief
Method: Each pair pastes its test into the chat; facilitator picks two to discuss
Purpose: Compare solutions and spot common mistakes.
4C Phase: C3 Concrete Practice
Learning Objectives: LO3

Activity: 8 Commitment: one test this week
Duration: 6 min
Type: Reflection
Method: Individual note, then one sentence each in the chat
Purpose: Turn the session into one concrete next step.
4C Phase: C4 Conclusions
Learning Objectives: LO4

Activity: 9 Questions and close
Duration: 4 min
Type: Closing
Method: Open questions
Purpose: Clear remaining doubts and end on time.
4C Phase: C4 Conclusions
