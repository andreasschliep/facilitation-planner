## 4. Session blocks

One section per phase, opened by its acceptance sentence. Every block uses
the same seven prompts:

### <time> · <block title> (<minutes> min)

- **Purpose:** why this block exists
- **Method:** what participants do
- **Materials:** what you need at hand
- **Say this:** the instruction, word for word
- **Watch out:** what typically goes wrong and how you notice it
- **Debrief:** the questions that turn the activity into learning
- **Transition:** one sentence that leads into the next block

After writing, read every paragraph against its phase's acceptance sentence.
If a paragraph does not help participants get there, cut it.
